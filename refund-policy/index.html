Refund Policy

Last updated 2026-11-26

Paddle is the Merchant of Record for Oscinote Professional purchases and processes refund requests. Refund eligibility and applicable deadlines are described in Paddle’s Refund Policy and applicable law.

How to request a refund

Use the support link in your Paddle receipt or visit Paddle buyer support and choose “Request refund”. Have the receipt or order number and purchase email ready. For problems installing or activating the app, Contact Oscinote.

What happens after a refund

A full refund revokes the licence. Your local recordings and project files stay on your computer, where you can still export them.

Your mandatory consumer rights still apply, including applicable withdrawal rights and remedies for faulty or misdescribed software.
