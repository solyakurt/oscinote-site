Oscinote Professional

Tools for focused practice

Oscinote is installable desktop software for Windows and Linux. Tune, record and analyze a single melody, then work on a phrase with loops, reference audio and measured feedback.

TunerRecordingAnalysis & CoachPracticeCompareProjects

Precision tuner

Choose from 31 instrument profiles across 8 families, including guitar, bowed strings, winds and brass. Tune in automatic, manual or chromatic mode. String profiles offer tuning presets and custom tunings. You can hear reference tones, select a reference A4, and display written pitches for transposing instruments.

See the instrument list and bench measurements for ranges and measurement limits.

Record and export takes

Record a mono track with input levels and clipping indicators. Each take keeps the instrument and tuning used to record it. Import WAV recordings, export 24-bit WAV files and save project backups locally.

Pitch analysis and Smart Practice Coach

Follow pitch over time, inspect individual notes in cents and see which parts of the take the analyzer can measure confidently. The Coach can highlight pitch drift and unstable held notes. A lesson score or an attached monophonic MIDI file gives it a reference for identifying mismatched notes and timing where enough clear notes align.

Open a suggested drill or send the highlighted phrase to Practice with a loop and a slower starting speed.

Practice with a score and a loop

Choose an A–B section, slow it down with pitch preserved and use a metronome or count-in. The library contains 128 exercises across instrument profiles. Lessons offer a playable reference with synchronized notation, tablature for supported fretted instruments, and results from recorded attempts.

Compare two takes

Record a retake directly from Practice. Compare the same passage on overlaid pitch graphs and move between the original and retake on the aligned timeline. Oscinote reports intonation changes beyond the measurement margin and compares rhythm when both takes can be matched reliably to a shared score.

Projects, practice history and updates

Saved takes, settings and practice history stay on your computer. Export a project backup, a take as WAV or practice history as CSV. Settings also includes optional checks for app updates.

Languages

English, Ukrainian, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Polish and Japanese. Switch in Settings at any time.
