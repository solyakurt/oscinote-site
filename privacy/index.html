Who is responsible

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, Contact Oscinote.

Recordings and projects

Oscinote uses the microphone while you tune or record. Your recordings, analysis, projects, settings and practice history stay on your computer. The app processes them locally and has no app account or analytics.

Trial and licence

When you start a trial, activate a key or check licence status, Oscinote sends a random installation identifier and a one-way hash of your computer’s identifier. Activation and status checks also send the key or licence identifier when needed. The service keeps trial dates, activations, licence status and Paddle transaction and customer identifiers. It uses a keyed hash of the request’s network address to limit repeated trial requests.

These records let us deliver licences, apply trial and activation limits and handle refunds. Licence and trial records are kept while needed to manage those entitlements, enforce the limits or comply with legal obligations.

Website and downloads

GitHub Pages serves the website and update files. Cloudflare hosts the licence service. Like other web requests, visits and update checks disclose your network address and request information to the hosting provider. The website’s analysis demo uses sample recordings provided on the page. It does not open your microphone.

Payments and browser storage

Paddle processes purchases, billing information, receipts and refunds under its own Privacy Policy. Payment card details go directly to Paddle. Checkout loads Paddle.js. After buying through the website, the checkout tab temporarily stores a random code so it can retrieve the licence key. The main website has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Support and your rights

If you contact us, we use your email and the information you share to respond and resolve the issue. Where applicable, you can ask to access, correct or delete personal data, object to or restrict processing, request a copy, or complain to your data protection authority. If your request concerns Paddle’s payment records, contact Paddle buyer support.
