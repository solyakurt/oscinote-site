How to read it

Cents (¢)
A hundredth of a semitone. +12 ¢ means the note sounds 12 hundredths of a semitone above the nearest note of the scale, measured from the stated A4.
Pitch reference
The A4 frequency the notes are measured against. Syrinx is measured at 442 Hz, the pitch the flutist tuned to; the period flute at 415 Hz, the usual baroque pitch.
Spread
How much a held note wavers: the range covered by the middle half of its pitch readings. Above 15 ¢ the note is marked as wavering.
Vibrato
A regular wave in the pitch, given as its rate in hertz and its width in cents. Vibrato is a choice, so it is shown, never marked as an error.
Grey stretches
Moments the analyser could not follow with confidence, such as breaths and fast changes. They are left out of every figure.
