Installing

  1. Run the installer. It installs Oscinote for your user account; no administrator rights are needed.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway.
  3. Allow microphone access in Settings → Privacy & security → Microphone, including for desktop apps.

Starting your trial

After installing, open the app and choose “Start the 7-day free trial”. An internet connection is needed to start. The trial lasts 168 hours and the tools work offline after it starts. Settings → License shows the time left.

Installing with a licence

After purchase, open Settings → License and enter your licence key while online. You can activate up to 3 computers. See pricing and licence details.

Automatic update checks

Release builds check for signed updates in the background. When an update is available, choose “Update and restart” to download and install it from the app. You can manage update checks in Settings → Updates.
