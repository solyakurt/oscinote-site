How it works

  1. Tune

    The string you play lights up, with the way to turn.

  2. Record

    One track, with a meter that warns before it clips.

  3. Analyze

    Every note’s pitch in cents, on a curve over time.

  4. Practice

    Loop the hard bar at 70 %; the pitch stays put.

  5. Compare

    Note by note, against your last take.

  6. Save

    Projects on your computer, exportable any time.

1. Tune

Choose your instrument, press Start tuner and play one note. The microphone is used only after you press Start.

2–3. Record and analyse

Record a take, then read its pitch curve and the measured offset of each note.

4. Practise

Loop the hard part and slow it down; the pitch stays the same.

5. Compare

Compare two takes note by note.

6. Save

Everything is saved to projects on your computer as you go.
