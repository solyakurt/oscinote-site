Frequently asked questions

Does Oscinote upload my recordings?

No. Audio, projects, settings and practice history stay on your computer. There is no account, no analytics and no upload.

Does it work offline?

Starting a trial and activating a licence need an internet connection. Afterwards, tuning, recording, analysis, practice, exercises, comparison and Help work offline.

When is the microphone used?

Only after you press Start tuner or Record. Stop releases it immediately. Your operating system asks for permission the first time.

Which instruments are supported?

Guitars (including 7/8/12-string and baritone), basses (4/5/6-string, fretless), ukulele, mandolin, banjo, violin, viola, cello, double bass, many winds and brass, piano single notes and voice, plus a chromatic profile for anything else. Full list.

Can it analyse chords?

No. Analysis is one note at a time. Chords and several instruments can be recorded and played back; the analysis says they cannot be transcribed.

How accurate is it?

On synthetic test tones the tuner reads within ±1 cent and note medians in the analysis within 3 cents; real instruments read less steadily. Details.

Does it judge my playing?

No. It shows measured quantities and their uncertainty. Uncertain detection is a limit of the measurement, never presented as your mistake.

Is there a free trial?

Yes: every feature for exactly 7 days (168 hours), starting when you start the trial in the app. No credit card or payment details are needed.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing is charged. The tools require a licence. You can still open your saved projects and export backups and WAV files.

How do I get help?

See Contact for app support, privacy and payment questions.

What does it cost?

A one-time $19.99 USD for the desktop app. No subscription and no automatic payments. One licence works on up to 3 of your computers.

Where is my data stored?

In the app’s data folder on your computer. Each project can be exported as one backup file.
